The Private Equiter

Short videos about private equity, M&A and how companies were built.

PE Uploader is a small desktop tool used by The Private Equiter to send its own finished videos to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts. The creator reviews, adds the caption and publishes each video manually in the TikTok app.

  • Uses TikTok Login Kit (desktop) and the Content Posting API (upload to inbox, scope video.upload).
  • Only uploads videos produced by The Private Equiter.
  • Never publishes automatically and never reads other users' data.
