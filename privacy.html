Privacy Policy — PE Uploader

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Data we access. With your permission, PE Uploader receives an access token for your TikTok account with the scopes user.info.basic (your display name and open ID) and video.upload (to send videos to your inbox).

How it is used. Only to upload the videos you select and to show which account is connected.

Storage. The token is stored only on the local computer that runs the tool. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API. We do not sell, share or analyse your data.

Retention and deletion. Deleting the local token file, or revoking access in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services), removes access immediately.

Contact. Through The Private Equiter channel on YouTube or TikTok.
